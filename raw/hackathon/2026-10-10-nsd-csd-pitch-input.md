## Part 2: NSD objective example, "Enrichment Meeting Pitch Script" (verbatim paste)

Enrichment Meeting Pitch Script

1. Opening
● Namaste [Customer Name] ji, main [Your Name] baat kar raha hoon
IndiaMart se.
● Sir, main aapka dedicated Account Manager hoon, jo company ne
specifically assign kiya hai aapki profile improve karne ke liye
● Aapki firm [Company Name] hamare saath registered hai, usi ke Profile
Update ke regarding aur Portal/Control Panel Overview ke liye call kiya
hai.
● Sir, maine aapka catalog check kiya, usme abhi aapke products aur
shop ki images missing hain. Is wajah se aapko buyers ki calls kam
aa rahi hongi.
2. Agenda
● Sir, IndiaMART par laakhon supplier profiles hain. Lekin company ne is
mahine mein sirf 500 selected companies ka profile deeply theek karne
ka initiative start kiya hai.
● Aapki company unhi selected profiles mein hai. Mera kaam hai
personally ensure karna ki aapki free profile visibility badhao
● Main 3 cheezon mein personally aakar aapki help karoonga:
○ Profile Setup: Aapke products ki normal photos click karke sahil
category aur keywords update karunga.
○ Portal/Control Panel Training: IndiaMART par buyer enquiries
kaise aati hain, kaun genuine hoti hain aur unhe reply kaise karna
hai — live dikhaunga.
○ Visibility: Aapki profile ko verify karke improve karenge taaki
aapke business ki visibility improve ho
3. Setting the Appointment (The Commitment)
● Iske liye mujhe aapse short face-to-face meeting karni hogi.
● Sirf 30 minute lagenge — photos, verification aur portal ka overview
aapke saath baithkar discuss karunga
● Kal morning 10-11 baje fresh mind se milte hain

Refined Objection Handling
Objection 1: "Mujhe koi package nahi lena, paise kharch nahi karne."
● Response: "Sir, main abhi kisi package ki baat hi nahi kar raha. Pehle
jo aapka free profile hai, usko toh theek se setup kar lein! Jab tak aap
portal ka overview nahi lenge aur photos nahi lagengi, tab tak aapko
response nahi dikhega. Main bas aapki existing listing improve karne aa
raha hoon."
● (Note: This keeps the door open for sales during the meeting without
promising "no sales" ever.)
Objection 2: "Main bahut busy hoon, phone par hi bata do."
● Response: "Sir, phone par main aapko sirf bata sakta hoon, karke nahi
dikha sakta. Portal ke features aur photography ke liye mera wahan
hona zaroori hai. Business mein aap busy hain, isiliye main chahta
hoon ki hum ek fixed time rakhein taaki main aapka poora setup 40
minute mein khatam kar doon."
Objection 3: "Aap aakar kya alag kar doge? Main khud photo daal
loonga."
● Response: "Bilkul daal sakte hain sir, par humein pata hota hai ki
IndiaMart ke algorithm ke hisaab se kaunsi details aur keywords zaroori
hain. Plus, main aapko portal ka jo overview dunga, usse aapko
enquiries manage karne mein bahut aasani hogi. Ek baar mil lete hain,
aapka kaam asaan ho jayega."

Key Success Mantras for this Pitch
● Don't use the word "Sales": Use words like Service, Verification,
Profile Update, Support, and Cataloging.

Strategy for the Meeting

As an executive, your mindset during the meeting should be:
1. Start with the Free Value: Click normal photos of
products/infrastructure and update the catalog.
2. Give the Portal Overview: While updating, show them the message center,
how to respond to buyers, and how many people are searching for their
products, the kind of enquiries (buy leads) are available for you.
3. Show Competition: While showing the catalog, show how their catalog
would be visible, how products show up on search and how competitors
have been with Indiamart for XX years and paid XX amount.
4. The Pivot: Once they see the high search volume and how easy the
portal is to use, say: "Sir, abhi itne log search kar rahe hain toh aapko
2-3 enquiry aa rahi hai, agar hum isse [Paid Feature] se boost kar dein,
toh ye count kaafi badh sakta hai."
5. Let them ask: By showing them the "Gaps" in their free profile versus a
paid package, make them curious about the paid package.
