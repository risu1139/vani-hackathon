## Rule
1. The competitor or social-proof line ("sellers like you are using these leads / getting this business") is an important hook, and we want it.
2. It must refer to **peer sellers of the same size**: the same turnover band and a similar AOV, in the same category (MCAT), and preferably the same city or region.
   - If a small seller hears about big sellers, they answer "those are big distributors, I don't compete with them".
   - If a big seller hears about small ones, they answer "they're not like me".
3. VANI may only say it when the numbers come from data, i.e. from `verified_facts`. Today we have competitor names, local lead counts and paid-seller counts (E08), but not which leads competitors bought or what they earned. A peer-group statistic has to be built first, for example "N sellers in your category and turnover band in your city bought M buy leads last month". Until it exists, the line is not allowed.
